Build a responsive web application called “VentureForge” — a marketplace for business ideas and early-stage venture development.

The platform allows users to submit business ideas, vote on them, contribute to them, and express interest in joining the idea in different roles such as angel investor, CTO, CFO, CMO, legal advisor, product contributor, operations lead, or general contributor.

The website should be beginner-friendly, clean, modern, and professional. It should feel like a mix between Product Hunt, LinkedIn, and an early-stage startup incubator platform.

Core concept:
Each business idea is treated as an evolving venture opportunity. An idea can start as a simple concept and mature over time toward launch, revenue, scale, acquisition, or IPO readiness.

Main user roles:
1. Visitor
   - Can browse public business ideas.
   - Can view rankings, categories, maturity level, geography, and required contributions.
2. Registered user
   - Can submit business ideas.
   - Can upvote or downvote ideas.
   - Can comment on ideas.
   - Can show interest in contributing to an idea.
   - Can add suggestions or improvements to the concept.
3. Idea owner
   - Can create and manage their own business idea.
   - Can define maturity level, required contributors, required venture elements, locations, and product-market fit assumptions.
   - Can accept or reject contribution requests.
4. Admin
   - Can moderate users, ideas, comments, flagged content, and inappropriate submissions.

   - Search bar for finding business ideas.
   - Featured business ideas.
   - Trending ideas based on votes and activity.

Business idea maturity model:
Each idea should have a maturity stage. Use the following stages:
1. Raw concept
2. Problem validated
3. Solution defined
4. Market research completed
5. Business model drafted
6. MVP in development
7. MVP launched
8. Early traction
9. Revenue generating
10. Scaling
11. Investment ready
12. Acquisition or IPO path

Venture elements:
Each business idea should have a checklist of required venture elements. These are things needed to move the idea forward. Include:
- Business plan
- Market research
- Product-market fit validation
- MVP
- Technical architecture
- Financial model
- Legal structure
- Founder agreement
- Contracts
- Brand identity
- Website or landing page
- Sales strategy
- Marketing strategy
- Operations plan
- Funding plan
- Location or operating jurisdiction
- Regulatory review
- Intellectual property review

Each venture element should have a status:
- Not started
- Needed
- In progress
- Completed
- Needs expert review

Product-market fit:
Each idea should allow location-specific product-market fit notes. For example, the same idea may have different assumptions in South Africa, Brazil, Europe, or the United States due to laws, competition, culture, infrastructure, pricing, customer behaviour, or market maturity.

Create a section on each idea called “Market Fit by Region”.
For each region, show:
- Country or region
- Market opportunity
- Key customer segment
- Local competitors
- Legal or regulatory considerations
- Barriers to entry
- Pricing assumptions
- Product changes needed for that market
- Product-market fit confidence score from 1 to 5

Voting:
Users can upvote or downvote business ideas.
A user can only vote once per idea.
Show total score and number of votes.

Contribution interest:
Users can click “Offer Contribution” on an idea and choose:
- Role they want to play
- Short message
- Relevant experience
- Whether they want to contribute for free, for equity, for payment, or as an investor

Important:
Do not build payment processing in the first version.
Do not allow actual investment transactions in the first version.
Include a clear disclaimer that the platform does not provide financial, legal, or investment advice.
Include a disclaimer that users must do their own due diligence before entering into any commercial, legal, or investment arrangement.

Recommended technical approach:
Use a simple full-stack web app suitable for Replit.
Use React for the frontend.
Use a clean component-based design.
Use a backend API.
Use a database to store users, ideas, votes, comments, contribution requests, venture elements, and market-fit regions.
Use Replit’s built-in database or PostgreSQL if available.
Include simple authentication for user accounts.
Use responsive design so it works on desktop and mobile.

- Minimal clutter
